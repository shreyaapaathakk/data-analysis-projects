## Business Insights and Recommendations

### Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data containing 9,994 records and 21 columns to evaluate sales performance, profitability, product categories, regional performance, discounts, and monthly trends using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib.

### Key Findings

**1. Overall Business Performance**
- Total sales: $2,297,200.86
- Total profit: $286,397.02
- Total orders: 5,009
- Total customers: 793
- Overall profit margin: 12.47%

**2. Category Performance**
- Technology generated the highest sales ($836,154.03) and profit ($145,454.95).
- Office Supplies generated $122,490.80 in profit.
- Furniture generated $741,999.80 in sales but only $18,451.27 in profit, indicating a comparatively low profit margin of 2.49%.

**3. Regional Performance**
- West was the strongest region, generating $725,457.82 in sales and $108,418.45 in profit.
- East ranked second in both sales and profit.
- Central had the lowest regional profit margin at approximately 7.92%, suggesting an opportunity to investigate costs, discounts, and product mix.

**4. Discount Analysis**
- Transactions with no discount generated $320,987.60 in profit.
- The 30% discount group recorded a loss of $10,369.28.
- The 40% discount group recorded a loss of $23,057.05.
- The 70% and 80% discount groups also showed substantial losses.

These findings indicate an association between higher discount levels and weaker profitability. Further analysis is needed to determine the contribution of product mix, costs, and other factors.

**5. Product-Level Performance**
- Copiers generated the highest sub-category profit at $55,617.82.
- Phones and Accessories were also major profit contributors.
- Tables recorded the largest sub-category loss at $17,725.48.
- Bookcases and Supplies also recorded negative profits.

**6. Monthly Sales and Profit Trends**
- Highest sales month: November 2017 — $118,447.82.
- Lowest sales month: February 2014 — $4,519.89.
- Highest profit month: December 2016 — $17,885.31.
- Lowest profit month: January 2015 — a loss of $3,281.01.

The results demonstrate that the months with the highest sales and highest profit were different, highlighting the importance of monitoring profitability alongside revenue.

### Business Recommendations

1. **Review discount policies:** Evaluate high-discount transactions and establish appropriate discount limits based on product-level profitability.
2. **Investigate Furniture profitability:** Examine pricing, discounts, and costs for Tables and Bookcases to identify potential improvements.
3. **Learn from high-performing products:** Investigate the factors behind Copiers, Phones, and Accessories profitability.
4. **Improve regional performance:** Investigate the Central region's lower profit margin and compare its product mix and discount practices with stronger regions.
5. **Monitor monthly KPIs:** Track sales, profit, and profit margin together to identify changes in performance over time.
